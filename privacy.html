Moon TV privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Moon TV is a player for a privately run media server. It shows what that server provides: live TV, recordings, films and series. It has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and no account with the developer.

Who the app talks to

Moon TV talks to the server you sign in to. Your sign-in, what you watch and the settings saved with your account go to that server and to no one else.

On your home network, the app looks for servers to sign in to and for screens to play on. That stays on your network.

A few optional features fetch public pictures or videos straight from the sites that publish them: trailers from YouTube, artwork from The Movie Database, book covers from Open Library and theme files from GitHub. Those sites see your device's internet address, as any website would. Moon TV asks them only for the picture or video it needs, and tells them nothing about you or your account.

Voice search, when you use it, turns your words into text with Apple's speech recognition. On Apple TV this happens on the device; on iPhone and iPad Apple may do it on its own servers. Moon TV does not record or keep the audio.

In the demo, the films play from the public sites of their publishers (the Blender Foundation and Mux). The demo signs in to nothing and sends nothing anywhere else.

Diagnostic reports

If the app crashes, it saves a report on the device and sends it to the server you sign in to the next time it connects. You can turn this off in Settings, About, Diagnostics & Logging. A report of what the app was doing is sent only when you choose "Send report to server" there, and only to that same server. Reports go only to that server.

The server you sign in to

The server you sign in to holds the accounts, watch history and settings that the app needs to work. The developer of Moon TV also runs a server that people with an account on it sign in to. For those accounts, that server keeps three things: your user name and the account's number, so it knows who is signed in; what you watch and how far you got, so it can resume where you left off and show what is next; and crash and fault reports from the app, so faults can be fixed.

These are kept with your account and used only to make Moon TV work. They are not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with anyone else, and not used to track you across other apps or websites. To have an account and what it holds removed, ask the person who gave you the account, or write to the support address.

If you sign in to a server someone else runs, the developer receives none of this; ask whoever runs that server what it keeps.

On your device

Settings, sign-in details, saved pictures and downloads stay on the device. Deleting the app removes them.

Children

Moon TV is not directed at children. Accounts are made by the person who runs the server, and the app keeps nothing beyond what is listed above for anyone, children included.
