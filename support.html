Moon TV support

Moon TV plays live TV, recordings, films and series from a privately run media server. It works with an account on that server, given to you by the person who runs it. There is no Moon TV account and nothing to buy.

Getting started

Open Moon TV and choose your server, or type its address, then sign in with the user name and password you were given. On Apple TV you can also sign in with a code from another device that is already signed in.

No server yet? Choose Try the demo on the first screen. The demo shows the app with free Creative Commons films from the Blender Foundation and needs no sign-in. To leave it, open Settings and choose Leave the demo.

Something is not working

If a title will not play or the guide is empty, the server is the first place to look: ask the person who runs it. If the app itself misbehaves, open Settings, About, Diagnostics & Logging and choose Send report to server, then tell the person who runs it. Reports go to that server only.

Contact

Questions or problems with the app: open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed). Please say which device you use (Apple TV, iPhone or iPad) and what you were doing. Requests on that page are public, so please leave out passwords, server addresses and anything personal.
